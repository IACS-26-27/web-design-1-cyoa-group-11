Ask for help

You tap the shoulder of the person beside you. They turn to you, confused. You signal them to be quiet.

You lean close, making sure your sister stays put.

"Please," you whisper, "help me. That man is going to hurt me."

Their eyes narrow in concern. They glance towards your father. Silently, they nod, standing up and walking to the far end of the subway. You can faintly hear them talking to another person.

Your father returns after a long, painful minute.

"Good." he mumbles. "You're listening."
