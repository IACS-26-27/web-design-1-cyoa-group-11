Stay where you are

You stay right where you are. You see no other option. Asking for help is too risky. The help might save you up until he comes back, or worse, the 'help' won't help at all.

You're stuck. You're stuck with a man who thinks love is measured in punches and slaps. A man who thinks you are merely a broken toy that needs fixing.

Your father returns. He pats you on the shoulder firmly.

"Good job. Be just as good in your work."

You nod and put your head down. Hopefully today he'll go easy on you.
