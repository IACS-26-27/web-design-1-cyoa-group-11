Peer Pressure

You were just going to class, and now you're in a crowd. Your peers are watching from every side. Dolly is on the floor, tears welling in her eyes. Ivy stands over her, seething. Ivy notices you first.

"ALLY! Tell this- this DOUCHEBAG that Axel isn't a joke!"

Dolly has started crying. Her eyes are shut tight, and her whole body's shaking.

"Please, Ally," she chokes out, "tell her to stop."

Your mind is racing. Axel is your friend. So is Ivy. You and Dolly broke up. But she looks so scared. What could she have possibly done?

Dolly never insults people.

What do you do? Protect Dolly or ask what happened.
