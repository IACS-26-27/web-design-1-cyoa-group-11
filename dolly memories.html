Memories

You're seated on the subway. Your father is tightly grasping your shoulder, making sure you don't run again. Your little sister is seated on your lap.

You know what's going to happen when you get home. Your sister shouldn't even know what the words he say mean. Neither should you.

Your father stands up. Before moving any further, he leans close.

"Stay there. Don't even try running from me."

You nod, shaking, as he walks towards the map of stops.

What now? Ask someone for help or stay where you are.
